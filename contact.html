Leaving a legacy gift

A gift in your Will to benefit the participants of the Veteran Games programme helps secure its future, giving future generations of veterans opportunities for physical activity, social connection and rehabilitation. To talk to the team about a legacy gift, please get in touch and we will call you back or arrange a meeting.
