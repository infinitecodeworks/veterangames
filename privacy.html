Privacy

This notice explains how the Veteran Games and Conference (Registered Charity No. 1202198) handles personal information in connection with this website.

What this website collects

This website does not use forms, cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Its fonts and images are served from the site itself rather than from third parties.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub logs the IP addresses of visitors for security and to keep the service running. GitHub’s handling of this data is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Members’ web app

The members’ sign-in page embeds the Military App web app. If you sign in or register, your information is handled by the Military App under its own privacy policy, available within the app.

Contacting us

If you email or phone us, we use the details you give us only to respond to your enquiry. You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing davidt@veterangames.co.uk or writing to Suite 3, One Bridge Lane, London NW11 0EA.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
