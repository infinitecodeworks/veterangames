The Veteran Games app

A private community space for Veteran Games participants and families, run on the Military App platform.

Connect

Keep the Veteran Games community together for current and previous participants, family and support team, with connection and regular engagement throughout the year.

Career and interest

Groups, resources and connections that may help you, whether with career decisions or a shared interest.

Support

Peer support, social engagement and access to welfare resources at the heart of the platform.

Family

Likeminded people from every year of the Veteran Games and Conference, with a shared set of values and respect.

Mobile app

Download the app for Android or iOS. You can find old friends and members near you, join Veteran Games groups, and take part in wider social, career, wellbeing and interest groups with events and chat.

Web app

If you prefer a laptop or home computer, the web app uses the same login and the same community. If you run a group or post regularly, the web app can make that quicker.

Sign in to the web app

Registering

To register you need the Veteran Games group access code. It has been shared by email and in the private Veteran Games social media groups, rather than in public.

If you don’t have the code, or need help at any stage, email the Military App help desk at help@militaryapp.org. You can also use the ‘Support and FAQ’ button at the bottom of the app’s sign-in screen.

Once registered, members have a private pinboard for photos and updates, private groups, events and chat, and a Support Suite with over 650 support videos and a library of more than 250 support charities.

Making the most of the app FAQs and support

The wider Military App community

You join through your Veteran Games groups, and can also connect with people, events and groups across the wider Military App community, including over 70 Walk+Talk groups and member-run Coffee Clubs across the UK and internationally.
